When building any persuasive deck/pitch that proposes spending money (an investment, a purchase, a plan with a price tag), **include the cost/price prominently — and anticipate it, don't bury it.** If you omit it, the audience's very first reaction is "¿pero cuánto cuesta?" They jump straight to the number, and the omission reads as the pitch dodging the crux.

**Why:** Real case (2026-06-01) — a couple-facing deck sold the dream beautifully (solar panels → free AC, no sacrifice) but left out the panel cost. The partner's immediate reply after seeing it: *"le faltó lo más importante… ¿cuánto cuestan los paneles?"* The persuasion momentum got spent answering a question the deck should have pre-empted, and the "reveal" lost its punch on a second send.

**How to apply:** In any investment/purchase pitch, dedicate a slide/section to **cost + payback/ROI + financing** (cost alone scares; cost + payback + "monthly ≈ what you already pay" reassures). Anticipate the "how much?" and answer it *before* they ask. The price is not the awkward thing to hide — it's the single most important thing the audience wants to know.
